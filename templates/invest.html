Pre-Answered Risks
Every Hard Question, Answered.
"How do you scale support without humans?"
AI support pipeline: 95% auto-resolved. Remaining 5% handled by the team + AI. Scales infinitely — each new ticket makes the AI smarter.
"What if CargoWise drops their price?"
They can't. WiseTech is publicly traded at $16B. Shareholders demand revenue growth. They just RAISED prices. Their cost structure cannot serve SMBs at $49.99.
"What about the team size?"
AI-native from day one. The multi-layer AI pipeline handles development, support, and operations autonomously. Codebase on GitHub, fully documented. 1-2 engineers join at $50K MRR for data ops and reliability.
"Where's your data at launch?"
Seeded from Maersk's free API (real-time spot rates) + public carrier schedules. User data compounds with every RFQ cycle. At 10K users, we have a data moat.
"Why can't a big company copy this?"
Networks are trade associations, not software companies. WCA has never built software. Carrier tools only show THEIR rates. Nobody aggregates across the ecosystem.
"$49.99 is too cheap. High churn?"
Stickiness: contacts + years of rate history + referral credits locked to the platform. Switching means losing your data, your benchmarks, and your free months. Slack is $7.25/user and has industry-low churn.
"Legal risk from data aggregation?"
Pivoted to partnerships + user-imported data + public sources only. GDPR-compliant with country-level email filtering. CAN-SPAM unsubscribe on all outreach. Opt-out tracking in database.
"What if AI tools change pricing?"
Multi-vendor strategy across diagnostic, engineering, and verification layers + open-source fallback. No single-vendor lock-in. AI costs are marginal vs subscription revenue.
